## Measuring the Business Impact of Agentic AI

**Speaker:** Dr. Siyka Andreeva (Business Value Manager, GitLab)

### Current Challenges

- 91% of AI leaders agree that the long-term introduction of AI will yield more results than tactical fixes
- The problem: organizations still face huge challenges
- 97% of AI complexity is operational, not technical
- When something is new, operational complexity is the problem
- We no longer fear AI, but we are under huge pressure to show measurable results
- Accountable for choices and decisions

### Rethinking ROI and Metrics

- Traditional ROI models and telemetry no longer work
- Let go of being overly detailed and precise
- Metrics like LOC no longer mean much
- Just ship it
- Review processes take time
- Looking at outcomes is the only way
- What your CFO wants to see
    - Hard cost savings
    - Productivity gains
    - Revenue growth
    - Risk mitigation
    - Innovation rate
- Some things won't change

### Calculating AI ROI in Practice

- Value is in the eye of the beholder
- Define value
    - Find a business problem worth solving
    - Focus on processes, not technology
    - Track the impact on your current KPIs
- Align value
    - Understand users and customers
    - Distinguish between high-value and low-value tasks
- Quantify value
    - Map tasks to business KPIs across the entire SDLC
- Establish a baseline and track the value

    - How big is the impact?
